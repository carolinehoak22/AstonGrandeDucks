Ducks of Aston Grande

I love the ducks that live here so much, and I hope you do too!! I made this website as a passion project to share photos of these ducks and to catalog the duck population in the neighborhood. No generative AI was used in this process, and I hope you enjoy my work 😀

The ducks that are shown here are muscovy ducks!! They love to eat peas, and fresh fruits, and some other types fo fresh veggies. They will also forage on their own, eating various plants, some bugs, and the occasional small fish. They shouldn't be fed avacados or bread, but peas are always a healthy favorite.
